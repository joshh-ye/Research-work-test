# Borzoi fine-tuning (FT) program: final report

Written 2026-10-25 by the computational engineer from existing files only (no new predictions, no new test evaluation). hg38 throughout. Every number below is taken from a file named next to it;
tables are in `reports/FINETUNING_COMPARISON.md`; regenerate the test numbers with `./torch-venv/bin/python analysis/ft/final_summary.py` (outputs in `results_ft/final/`).
Metric vocabulary: **Pearson r** = mean over tracks of per-track Pearson r pooled over all bins of a split; **r^2** = squared Pearson r (mean of per-track r^2); **prediction R^2** = 1 - SS_res/SS_tot (mean over tracks). They are different quantities and are labelled throughout.

## 1. Objective
Improve on the published system "E4 ridge[pre_raw:union1888]" (ridge on the four pretrained Borzoi replicates' output tracks; test r 0.7426 on 183 tracks, 0.7406 on the 152 non-identical tracks) for the 183 CRC ChIP/TF tracks,
by (i) cheap cache-based ridge/head optimisation, (ii) fine-tuning Borzoi trunks (frozen, partial, LoRA, full), (iii) replicate ensembles, test-time augmentation and blends. The program replaced the original Borzoi-TL head (test r 0.6820), which
the E4 analysis had shown to be below the ridge (`research_management/PROJECT_STATE.md`). Plan and pre-specified rules: `research_management/finetuning/FT_PLAN.md`.

## 2. Fixed evaluation contract (unchanged from E4, so numbers are comparable with 0.7426)
- Tiles from `results_phase2/trunk_cache/tiles.csv` (524,288 bp windows): train 4,659 / validation chr1+chr8 750 / test chr9+chr22 359. No split change (`split_hash 0513815cd86d3caf` in every checkpoint).
- Targets: the 183 cached BigWig tracks; evaluation on the shared 896 x 128 bp grid (crop 256 bins/side of the central 4,096 x 32 bp bins, mean-pool x4); same code path as E4 (`analysis/priorityD/cmp_lib.py`, `analysis/exposure_stratified/estats.py`).
- Claim set = 152 non-identical tracks (31 tracks are experiments identical to Borzoi/Enformer training data; `exposure_audit/track_exposure.tsv`, `Borzoi_class == identical_experiment`); all 183 reported alongside.
- Model, hyper-parameter, checkpoint and blend-weight selection used validation chromosomes only. Test predictions exist only for ids entered in `research_management/finetuning/TEST_LOCK.csv` and committed before evaluation; one evaluation per id.
- Uncertainty: 10,000 resamples, seed 20261020; TF-cluster (tracks resampled by TF, tiles fixed), tile-block (10-tile blocks), cell-line cluster, and two-way (tile blocks x TF, tile blocks x cell line) (`analysis/ft/compare_ft.py`).
- Claim rule (tightened and locked before any FT test run; `LOCK_PROPOSAL.md` rev 2, `FT_PLAN.md`): an improvement is claimed only for the PRIMARY config and only if, on the 152 tracks, the TF-cluster AND two-way (tile-block x TF) 95% CIs of delta r (primary - E4 ridge) exclude 0.
  Secondaries are reported descriptively and never ranked.
- MPRA data were not used for training, selection or tuning.

## 6. Conclusions (claim rule)
- **Primary (FT-T01) meets the pre-registered rule**: on the 152 non-identical tracks both the TF-cluster CI [0.0123, 0.0252] and the two-way tile x TF CI [0.0111, 0.0263] of delta r exclude 0. We therefore claim that the 8-member fine-tuned ensemble (replicate-specific trunks, RC TTA, no fitted weights)
  improves on the E4 ridge by delta r = +0.0173 (test r 0.7578 vs 0.7406, 152 tracks; +0.0160, 0.7586 vs 0.7426 on all 183). The tile-block and cell-line CIs also exclude 0. The comparison is system vs system (see limitations 3 and 4); the TF-cluster CI is conditional on the test regions.
- Secondaries are descriptive and not ranked. The 4-replicate partial ensemble (FT-T03, test r 0.7618 / 0.7613 on 183 / 152) scoring above the primary on test is **not** used for selection and does not change the locked primary; the differences among FT-T01, T03 and T04 (<= 0.0035 in delta r) were not formally compared (no paired CI between configurations); under the lock, secondaries are not ranked. They were near-tied on validation.
- The TTA-free ensemble (secondary FT-T02, +0.0121) still has both CIs above 0, but its two-way lower bound (0.0007) is close to 0; the RC-TTA contribution on test is +0.0049 (183) / +0.0051 (152) in r, descriptive only.
- The best cache ridge (FT-T05) adds +0.0061 [TF-cluster 0.0037, 0.0093] over E4, descriptive.
- Mechanism evidence is validation-only: unfreezing the trunk adds over the matched frozen control; ensembling replicates adds +0.0065 (4 replicates, 0.7475) to +0.0072 (8 members, 0.7482) over the best single model with TTA (FT-S03@rc 0.7410, 183 tracks); LoRA, partial and full fine-tuning are within about 0.003 of each other (single seed, seed noise about 0.0003 from one pair).

## 8. Limitations
1. **Exposure to pretrained-model training data.** About 44% of test base pairs lie in Borzoi training folds, and chr9/chr22 are largely in Borzoi/Enformer training folds (`exposure_audit/EXPOSURE_AUDIT.md`). The ridge reads the pretrained output heads, which include at least 31 experiments identical to project tracks; the fine-tuned models replace the head and partly retrain the trunk. The direction of the resulting bias is unknown and plausibly favours the ridge on the 183-track set, so the 152 non-identical tracks are the claim set. Both systems' trunks were trained on about 44% of the test base pairs, so the gain is shown on chr9/chr22 as they stand, not on sequence unseen by pretrained Borzoi.
   The direction of the resulting bias is unknown and plausibly favours the ridge on the 183-track set, which is why the 152 non-identical tracks are the claim set (the identical set is a lower bound: only HCT116/Caco-2 ENCODE cross-references were searched). The fine-tuned trunks themselves were pretrained on those experiments as well.
2. **Two test chromosomes** (359 tiles, chr9 + chr22): region-level uncertainty is substantial (tile-block CI of the absolute primary r is about 0.737-0.775); the TF-cluster CI conditions on these regions.
3. **Replicate-specific trunks vs a 4-replicate ridge.** The primary is an ensemble of eight models over four replicate trunks (partial + LoRA); the ridge uses the averaged 1,888 output channels of the four pretrained replicates. The comparison is of two systems, not of matched single components; the matched single-trunk controls exist on validation only.
4. **RC TTA asymmetry.** FT predictions average forward and reverse-complement passes; the ridge features come from the shift-emulated, no-RC cache. A TTA-free primary is provided (FT-T02, test r 0.7537 / 0.7527; delta +0.0121 with two-way lower bound 0.0007) so the TTA contribution can be separated.
5. **Validation optimism.** Early stopping per member on validation, TTA chosen on validation, primary chosen among near-tied ensembles (<= ~0.001), about 120 validation-evaluated configurations; blend weights fitted on validation. Absolute r is not comparable across splits (E4 ridge 0.7337 validation vs 0.7426 test); only the paired test deltas are free of selection bias (they remain subject to the exposure confounding in item 1).
6. **Single seed per member.** Seed noise was estimated from one pair (FT-S03 vs FT-S12, about 0.0003 on validation); differences among partial, LoRA and full arms (about 0.003) cannot be resolved with this evidence.
7. **MPRA.** MPRA data were not used for tuning; BL-007 (sign convention) remains open and no MPRA re-scoring of any fine-tuned model was done. The gains reported here are ChIP-track Pearson r only.
8. **Scope.** Poisson-loss MLP head only apart from FT-E1 (mse128); input context (524,288 bp), grid and splits were never changed; bf16 autocast was used (bf16 vs fp32 predictions r > 0.99; checkpoint resume exactness shown in fp32 and bf16 for partial mode on 12 tiles, not kill-tested for full/LoRA modes).
9. Checkpoint metadata records `git_commit` = HEAD at launch: `b2aa8de` for FT-S03 (launched before the FT code was committed in `9ed5985`) and `9ed5985` for FT-L1 and FT-R1..R3 (the only others inspected). The training code of FT-S03 therefore lived in the working tree; it is the committed `9ed5985` version that is archived. Non-TTA validation prediction via `predict_ft.py` asserts reproduction of each run's logged best validation r to 1e-3.
