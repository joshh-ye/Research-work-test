## Fig 1 - Model comparison (fig1_model_comparison)
Mean per-track **Pearson r** (a) and paired difference Δr = r(Borzoi-TL) − r(model) (b) on test chr9+chr22 (359 tiles, shared 128 bp grid), 183 CRC TF ChIP tracks; strata: all 183, primary 152 (non-identical experiment), identical-experiment 31. Pretrained models are shown with the oracle mapping (best test track per target; optimistic, hollow) and the validation-selected mapping (chr1/chr8; filled). Dashed line in (a) = Borzoi-TL. CIs in (b): 95% percentile bootstrap (10,000 resamples) over contiguous 10-tile blocks (thick), TF clusters (medium; 77/62/25 clusters) and cell-line clusters (dotted; 12/11/3 clusters), as stored in the source files. The identical-31 stratum contains experiments that were Borzoi/Enformer training data (24 LoVo GSM + ENCSR); AlphaGenome identity is unverified (proxy). Sources: results_phase2/exposure_stratified/exposure_stratified_stats.csv (tile_set all_359_tiles), results_phase2/e7_nonoracle/{enformer,alphagenome}/{delta_stats,model_summary}.csv. Data: figures/data/fig1_model_comparison.csv. Pearson r only (no R²) is plotted.

## Fig 4 - Variant provenance flow (fig4_variant_flow)
Counts of CRC fine-mapped credible-set variants (Chen 2024 S6, hg19 source; S6_CCV input hg38): 5,741 CCVs → 5,739 input rsIDs (2 lost to rsID merge/reassignment) → 10,148 input rows → 5,729 allele-matched (ALT = GWAS-reported allele; no strand flips) / 4,396 non-reported ALT (exploratory) / 23 no allele info; 4,323 of the matched variants tested in MPRA (134 positives / 4,098 negatives / 91 excluded; 1,406 untested); 58 informative regions. Counts are recomputed from files and asserted. Sources: variant_provenance/*.tsv, results_phase2/e2_validation/labels/label_counts.tsv. No statistic is shown.

## Fig 5 - E2 MPRA validation, within-region P1 AUROC (fig5_e2_p1)
**INTERIM, unadjusted, Holm deferred; Borzoi/Borzoi-TL pending.** Stratified within-region AUROC (Mann-Whitney; not r/r²/R²) of AlphaGenome (quantile score) and Enformer vs trivial baselines (distance to TSS, Chen annotation count); 130 positives / 2,518 negatives in 58 informative regions (4,232 variants).
