## Title

Leakage-aware evaluation of genomic foundation models for colorectal cancer risk-variant annotation and transcription-factor binding <!-- src: research_management/SCIENTIFIC_DECISIONS.md SD-020 (B title), SD-024 (Title = SD-020 B) -->

Note: this framing was chosen by the pre-registered rule (SD-024), not by the authors' preference.

## Abstract

**Background.** Fine-mapping of colorectal cancer (CRC) GWAS loci defines 5,741 credible causal variants (CCVs), mostly noncoding. Sequence-to-function models can score them, but benchmarks accounting for training-data exposure and independent functional data are lacking.
**Methods.** AlphaGenome, pretrained Borzoi, Enformer and Borzoi-TL (a frozen-trunk head on 183 CRC TF ChIP-seq tracks) were scored under one protocol frozen before any label was joined. The pre-registered primary test (P1) was within-locus stratified AUROC (not r, r^2 or R^2) for MPRA-active versus inactive variants (Law et al.; 130 positives, 2,518 negatives, 58 informative regions), with region-clustered bootstrap, permutation p, Holm over four models, and distance and annotation-count baselines. Track prediction was benchmarked on 359 test tiles against a ridge readout and a linear probe (TF-cluster bootstrap), with a training-exposure audit.
**Results.** P1 AUROC: Enformer 0.674 [0.607, 0.756], AlphaGenome 0.666 [0.590, 0.759], Borzoi-TL 0.661 [0.584, 0.768], pretrained Borzoi 0.640 [0.554, 0.744]; Holm p = 0.0004 for each. Distance to TSS gave 0.515 [0.445, 0.567] and the Chen annotation count 0.618 [0.556, 0.684]. All models exceeded distance; none was shown to exceed annotation count, and no model differed from another (all paired CIs include 0). Direction concordance (P2) was below 0.5 but is uninterpretable: the MPRA sign convention is unresolved. For track prediction, a ridge readout and a linear probe exceeded Borzoi-TL on 152 non-identical tracks (TL minus ridge -0.0555 [-0.0756, -0.0428]; TL minus probe -0.0378 [-0.0525, -0.0289]; TF-cluster CIs). At least 31 of 183 test tracks are Borzoi/Enformer training experiments, and chr9/chr22 lie largely in their training folds.
**Conclusions.** Within loci, all four models rank MPRA-active variants above distance, comparably to each other and to existing annotation counts; no tumour-specific signal was shown. The original head-only fine-tuning recipe did not beat linear readouts. No causal or clinical claim is made.

## 1. Introduction

**1.1 CRC genetics.** [Colorectal cancer is heritable; GWAS have identified many risk loci (huyghe2019). Trans-ancestry fine-mapping of over 254,000 individuals defined 238 independent signals in 142 regions and 5,741 credible causal variants (chen2024); the CCV definition is variants whose conditional P is within two orders of magnitude of the signal's most significant variant (no posterior inclusion probability exists; `variant_provenance/PROVENANCE.md`). Most CCVs are noncoding. High-throughput MPRA has tested 8,880 candidate variants in colon cell lines (law2024).]
**1.2 Sequence-to-function models.** [Basenji (kelley2018), Enformer (avsec2021), Borzoi (linder2025) and AlphaGenome (avsec2026) predict chromatin, TF-binding and expression tracks from sequence and are used to score variants. Their training data (ENCODE, GEO, GTEx) overlap many public CRC assays; this makes test-set exposure a first-order concern for any downstream benchmark. Cross-individual variant-effect prediction is known to be limited (huang2023).]
**1.3 Transfer learning.** [Adapting a pretrained model to a disease-specific track panel by retraining a head has been shown for Enformer on breast and prostate cancer TF ChIP-seq (li2026) and parameter-efficient fine-tuning for Borzoi-family models has been described (yuan2026). Genomic augmentation is reviewed in lee2023. Novelty of the method itself is limited; the contribution here is the leakage-aware evaluation, a supervised-baseline test of whether head-only fine-tuning exceeds a linear readout (it did not, for the one recipe tested), and the variant-annotation validation.]
**1.4 Aims.** [(i) Benchmark a frozen-trunk Borzoi head (Borzoi-TL) against pretrained Borzoi, Enformer and AlphaGenome and against supervised linear readouts (ridge on pretrained outputs; linear probe on the trunk embedding) on 183 CRC TF tracks with exposure-stratified inference. (ii) Score CRC CCVs with the same models under one frozen protocol and test, within locus, whether scores discriminate MPRA-active from inactive variants beyond trivial baselines.]
Note for authors: the Introduction sentences above are outlines. Only the citations in `references.tsv` may be used; no other reference has been verified.

### 2.3 Tiles and splits
The genome is cut into non-overlapping 524,288 bp windows from position 0 (`borzoi_code/genome_tiler.py`). Training: all other assembled chromosomes (4,659 tiles); validation chr1 + chr8 (750 tiles); test chr9 + chr22 (359 tiles) <!-- exposure_audit/EXPOSURE_AUDIT.md §4 (tile counts reconstructed from hg38.ml.fa.fai; 359 matches the known test count) -->. Targets are the 32 bp-binned signal over the central 131,072 bp (4,096 bins); inputs are one-hot 524,288 bp <!-- borzoi_code/dataset.py:10 -->. Evaluation on a shared 128 bp grid (4-bin means) is used for cross-model comparison; native-grid (32 bp) numbers are reported separately and never mixed with shared-grid numbers.
**These chromosomes are not held out from the pretrained backbones.** Test chr9/chr22 tiles: 43.6% of bp lie in Borzoi training folds (30.4% in the Borzoi test fold) and 73.0% in Enformer training folds; validation tiles chr1/chr8: 65.7% (Borzoi) and 89.7% (Enformer) in training folds <!-- exposure_audit/EXPOSURE_AUDIT.md §4 table; Enformer fold file is the third-party Genentech reproduction, Basenji2 first-party bed 0.726 per RV-004 -->. The chr9/chr22 test set is also development-exposed (results were viewed during development; SD-009).

### 2.4 Borzoi-TL architecture and training (as actually run)
Four pretrained Borzoi replicates (`johahi/borzoi-replicate-0..3`, HF port via `borzoi-pytorch`) are frozen; the 1,920-channel trunk embedding that feeds `human_head` is captured with a forward pre-hook, averaged across the four replicates, centre-cropped to 4,096 bins, and passed to the only trainable module: `Linear(1920,1024) -> GELU -> Dropout(0.2) -> Linear(1024,183) -> Softplus` (2,154,679 parameters) <!-- borzoi_code/model.py; PHASE1_RECOVERY_REPORT.md §4 -->.
Training (Slurm 12212537, `main.py --epochs 8 --ckpt-dir ./borzoi_ckpt_full --output-dir ./results_full`, `training_run.slurm`): Adam on head parameters, lr 1e-4, weight decay 1e-4, batch size 1, Poisson negative log-likelihood (`log_input=False`), `ReduceLROnPlateau` (factor 0.5, patience 1), early-stopping patience 3, augmentation on training tiles only: random shift up to +/-128 bp snapped to whole 32 bp bins (+/-4 bins) and reverse-complement with p = 0.5 <!-- main.py:62-85, 265-282; borzoi_code/dataset.py:27-53 -->. **Not used:** inverse-signal per-track loss weighting (`--balance-tracks` was not passed), and no low-variance down-weighting (`low_variance_flag` is False for all 183 tracks) <!-- research_management/EXPERIMENT_REGISTRY.csv EXP-001; PHASE1_RECOVERY_REPORT.md §9 -->. The checkpoint used is epoch 5 of 8 (validation NLL 0.18523; epochs 6-8 did not improve) <!-- borzoi_ckpt_full/ (epoch 5); results_full/history.json -->. **The headline checkpoint is a single run; no seed was set; single train/val/test split.** Seed and chromosome-fold robustness were assessed afterwards with heads retrained on a slim cache whose recipe differs from the headline checkpoint (Section 2.5b). The choice of trunk embedding as the feature tap was not ablated; the same embedding is used by the linear probe (Section 2.5a). The Poisson-NLL MLP head, batch size 1, lr 1e-4, 8-epoch recipe is the only fine-tuning recipe tested; no alternative loss, optimiser, batch size or head architecture was evaluated on chr9/chr22 (SD-021) <!-- src: research_management/SCIENTIFIC_DECISIONS.md SD-020, SD-021 -->.

### 2.5a Supervised linear baselines (E4; EXP-013; rule pre-specified in SD-008, committed before results)
- **Ridge on pretrained outputs (primary).** Features = pretrained 4-replicate Borzoi outputs on the shared 896 x 128 bp grid, restricted to the "union1888" channels (1,882 ChIP-TF channels + 6 CRC DNase channels; SD-018), raw scale. Sensitivity variants: 1,882-channel ChIP subset, 132 CRC-track subset, each also with log1p transform. A ridge on all 7,611 pretrained tracks was planned but dropped with the slim cache (SD-018) and was not run. <!-- src: results_phase2/e4_ridge/SUMMARY.md; research_management/SCIENTIFIC_DECISIONS.md SD-018 -->
- **Linear probe (exposure-symmetric comparison).** Same ridge on the frozen 1,920-channel trunk embedding that the Borzoi-TL head consumes (`probe:trunk1920`), so it sees the same input as TL and no pretrained output vocabulary. <!-- src: results_phase2/e4_ridge/SUMMARY.md; research_management/REVIEW_FINDINGS.md RV-006 (probe to be foregrounded) -->
- **Fitting.** Training tiles = the same tiles as Borzoi-TL (all chromosomes except chr1, chr8 validation and chr9, chr22 test); features are standardised with train-only mean/SD; the ridge path is fitted from train-only X'X and X'Y; lambda is chosen on the validation chromosomes only (max mean per-track Pearson r) over the grid 1e-6, 1e-5, ..., 100 (nine values; grid from `analysis/priorityD/ridgelib.py`, line 9); no refit on train+validation; test chromosomes are evaluated once with the selected lambda. Selected lambda 1e-4 for ridge[pre_raw:union1888] and 0.01 for the probe, neither at a grid edge. <!-- src: results_phase2/e4_sensitivity/SUMMARY.md part B (code read of 03_ridge.py / ridgelib.py); results_phase2/e4_ridge/SUMMARY.md lambda table -->
- **Parity.** Cache targets equal `results_full/test_targets.npy` (max abs diff 0.0); the ridge, probe, pretrained and TL statistics use the same 359 tiles in the same order and the same per-track pooled-correlation code path. <!-- src: results_phase2/e4_sensitivity/SUMMARY.md part B -->
- **Pre-specified interpretation rule.** If TL minus the best pre-specified ridge has a CI that includes 0 or is negative, "CRC-specific adaptation" is not supported beyond a supervised linear readout (SD-008). The rule was triggered (SD-019).
- **Exposure.** The ridge features (1,888 channels) include Borzoi training tracks for >=31 of the 183 targets, so ridge and TL are stratified by Borzoi exposure class; the probe is the exposure-symmetric comparator. <!-- src: results_phase2/e4_ridge/SUMMARY.md limitations -->

### 2.5b Seed and chromosome-fold robustness (E5; EXP-015) and held-out-vocabulary transfer (E8; EXP-016)
- **Cache recipe (SD-018, storage-forced deviation).** The slim trunk cache keeps forward trunk embeddings, targets and pretrained Borzoi outputs for 1,888 channels; reverse-complement embeddings were not kept. Heads retrained for E5 and E8 therefore use the "cache recipe": identical hyperparameters to the headline run except (i) no reverse-complement augmentation, (ii) shift augmentation emulated on the cached embeddings, (iii) fp16 cached features. The original checkpoint remains the headline TL model; it was re-evaluated from the fp16 cache as a gate and reproduces the published statistics. <!-- src: research_management/SCIENTIFIC_DECISIONS.md SD-018; results_phase2/e5_seeds/SUMMARY.md; results_phase2/e4_ridge/SUMMARY.md (gate rows) -->
- **Seeds.** Five heads with explicit seeds 1-5 on the original split. Seeds vary head initialisation, dropout and data order/augmentation only; the frozen backbone and its exposure to chr9/chr22 are unchanged. <!-- src: results_phase2/e5_seeds/SUMMARY.md -->
- **Chromosome folds.** Folds F2 (test chr6, chr21), F3 (chr11, chr19, chr20) and F4 (chr7, chr18), each with its own validation chromosomes used only for early stopping, lambda and pretrained-track selection. Fold CV tests head robustness only; it does not remove backbone sequence exposure (SD-009 clarification). All nine fold x seed heads are complete (F2, F3, F4 x seeds 1-3; cache recipe). No linear-probe statistics exist for F2-F4. <!-- src: results_phase2/e5_folds/e5_fold_vs_ridge.csv (9 heads per track set) -->
- **E8 design (validation-selected same-TF transfer; not zero-shot).** One head (seed 1, cache recipe) is trained on 166 tracks, excluding the 17 tracks of GP5d, LS174T, LS180 and DLD-1. For each held-out track, a trained same-TF track of another cell line is selected using the held-out track's own validation-chromosome (chr1, chr8) labels (fallback rule: any trained track, 4 tracks), then linearly calibrated on validation; this is compared with the pretrained Borzoi validation-selected mapping (1,882 any-TF ChIP channels). Because validation labels of the held-out tracks are used, this is not zero-shot, and the selection freedom of the two arms differs. <!-- src: results_phase2/e8_generalisation/SUMMARY.md -->

### 2.6 Training-exposure audit
Track identity was resolved from GEO text (179 GSM, 0 errors) and ENCODE REST (4 ENCSR; ENCODE `dbxrefs` GEO:GSM to ENCSR for HCT116/Caco-2). A TL target is **identical-experiment** if its GSM/ENCSR is present in the pretrained model's target vocabulary file paths. Result: 31 tracks = 24 LoVo GSE51142 (GSM) + 4 colon ENCSR (head indices 145-148) + 3 HCT-116 ENCSR (ENCSR000DTO CTCF, ENCSR000BML POLR2A, ENCSR000BSA JUND); same set for Borzoi and Enformer; a **lower bound** (only HCT116/Caco-2 ENCODE cross-references searched; reprocessed duplicates not detected; AlphaGenome manifest not obtained, so AlphaGenome exposure is undetermined) <!-- exposure_audit/EXPOSURE_AUDIT.md §1 -->. Exposure class hierarchy: identical > same cell line + same target > same cell line other target > same target other cell line > unexposed (Borzoi counts 31/22/112/13/5) <!-- exposure_audit/EXPOSURE_AUDIT.md §2 --> (the unexposed classes use cell-line-level regex matching and are descriptive). Primary TL analysis set = the 152 non-identical tracks (SD-014), all 183 secondary.

### 2.7 Metrics
Three quantities are distinct and always labelled:
- **Pearson r** (primary): per-track correlation over all test bins (pooled over tiles), averaged over tracks.
- **Squared Pearson r (r^2)**: the square of that correlation (per track, then averaged).
- **Prediction R^2 = 1 - SS_res/SS_tot** on the raw scale. For pretrained models this is **validation-calibrated R^2** (a linear map fitted on validation chromosomes, then applied to test); uncalibrated raw-scale R^2 of pretrained tracks is meaningless (previously observed -193) and is not reported. TL R^2 is uncalibrated on test, so TL-minus-baseline R^2 differences compare unlike objects and are secondary.
MPRA validation uses a different quantity, the stratified within-region AUROC, which is not r, r^2 or R^2. 51 of 359 test tiles have zero signal and inflate pooled correlations; a sensitivity analysis excludes them.

### 2.8 Bootstrap designs for model comparison
Inference units (SD-006): the **primary** inference unit is the **TF cluster**, on the primary analysis set of 152 non-identical-experiment tracks (`not_identical152`; all 183 secondary); the cell-line-cluster and tile-block CIs are reported alongside <!-- src: research_management/SCIENTIFIC_DECISIONS.md SD-006, SD-019, SD-023; RV-002 modification -->. Delta r = model A minus model B (tracks matched), 10,000 resamples, seed 20261020, percentile 95% CI <!-- results_phase2/exposure_stratified/SUMMARY.md; results_phase2/e4_sensitivity/SUMMARY.md part A -->: (a) track resampling (descriptive only; tracks are not independent); (b) **TF-cluster** (77 clusters; 62 for the 152-track set); (c) **cell-line-cluster** (12 labels incl. two tissue labels; 11 for the 152-track set); (d) **tile-block** (37 blocks of 10 contiguous tiles, within chromosome; only two chromosomes, so chromosome-level resampling is impossible); (e) **two-way** tile-block x cluster, with both models recomputed per resample. Cluster-level sign-flip tests on cluster-mean deltas (exact for <=16 clusters). Subsets with <6 clusters are descriptive only. Cell-line-cluster CIs rest on only 12 (11) clusters and are wide. For E4/E5, paired TL-minus-ridge and TL-minus-probe CIs use the same resampling indices and gate against the primary E4 tile-block CIs to 5e-5 <!-- src: results_phase2/e4_sensitivity/SUMMARY.md part A gate -->. The pretrained R^2 calibration is fixed and not resampled. The sign-flip test (unweighted mean of cluster means) and the CIs (track-weighted mean) estimate different quantities.

### 2.8b Post-primary-result sensitivity analyses (E4; EXP-014)
Two analyses were specified (SD-023) after the primary E4 result was known and are therefore labelled post-primary-result sensitivity analyses; they cannot overturn the pre-registered outcome (SD-019). <!-- src: research_management/SCIENTIFIC_DECISIONS.md SD-023; results_phase2/e4_sensitivity/SUMMARY.md; RV-006 #4 -->
- **Exposure-controlled ridge.** For each target, remove from the 1,888 feature channels every Borzoi track that is the identical experiment (same GSM or ENCSR) or a ChIP track of the same TF in the same cell line; targets with no such channel keep all channels; one lambda for all tracks chosen on validation. This is partial exposure control only: channels of other TFs and assays in the same cell line remain.
- **Fold stratification of test tiles.** Test tiles are split by overlap with Borzoi validation+test windows (`sequences_human.bed.gz`; held-out tile = >=50% of the evaluated central 131,072 bp in those windows) and per-track r is recomputed per group. Only Borzoi folds are used; Enformer fold exposure is not assessed here.

### 2.10 Variant scoring (frozen before any validation label was joined; SD-005, SD-014)
`variant_scoring/SCORING_SPEC.md`, track lists in `variant_scoring/track_lists/` <!-- src: variant_scoring/SCORING_SPEC.md -->. Windows: Borzoi 524,288 bp, Enformer 196,608 bp, AlphaGenome 1,048,576 bp, variant placed at window index L/2; windows running off a chromosome are N-padded for Borzoi/Enformer (149 / 30 rows) and shifted for AlphaGenome (242 rows / 147 positions, flagged). REF was asserted against the FASTA for every row (0 mismatches; 10,148/10,148) and alleles are never complemented <!-- src: SCORING_SPEC.md §1; research_management/EXPERIMENT_REGISTRY.csv EXP-008 -->. Aggregation: sum over the 512 bp centre (Borzoi 16 bins, Enformer 4 bins; AlphaGenome 501 bp mask, `DIFF_LOG2_SUM`), forward + reverse-complement averaged (Borzoi, Enformer; AlphaGenome RC handling is server-side and not controlled), pseudocount 1 on aggregated sums. Per-track signed effect lfc = log2((ALT+1)/(REF+1)). **Primary score** = max over frozen tracks of |lfc|; signed companion = lfc at the arg-max track; sensitivity = mean |lfc| over tracks. Track sets: Borzoi-TL 152 (31 identical-experiment tracks excluded; all 183 secondary); pretrained Borzoi and Enformer 132 keyword-selected CRC tracks (126 TF ChIP + 6 DNase; 118 LoVo, so the max is LoVo-weighted; histone ChIP, CAGE, RNA and non-malignant colon excluded); AlphaGenome 33 tracks (DNase 6, ATAC 1, ChIP-TF 26; HCT116 27). Cross-model comparison uses ranks (AUROC), not raw magnitudes. <!-- src: variant_scoring/SCORING_SPEC.md §2; E2_ANALYSIS_PLAN.md Amendment A1.8 -->

### 2.11 Validation design (SD-004, SD-016; `variant_scoring/E2_ANALYSIS_PLAN.md` with Amendments A1-A3, frozen before unblinding)
- **MPRA (primary)**: Law et al. ST2 (8,880 tested variants) and ST4 (275 hits, FDR < 1e-3 in HT29, SW403 or HCEC-1CT; REF/ALT and logFC) (law2024). Tested universe = matched CCVs in ST2 (4,323). Positive = ST4 (134), negative = ST2 flag 0 in all three lines (4,098); ST2 flag-1 "hit" (91) is excluded from the primary and positive in sensitivity S1 (A1.2). Allele orientation was checked against matched REF/ALT (132 kept, 2 swapped and excluded from P2, 0 dropped; A1.5, A2.3). The MPRA is episomal and per-variant effect sizes of non-hits are not public (EGA controlled), so a hit-versus-non-hit design is used. <!-- src: E2_ANALYSIS_PLAN.md §2a, A1, A2; results_phase2/e2_validation/labels/POWER.md -->
- **P1 (primary, pre-registered)**: within-region stratified AUROC (weights n_pos x n_neg; midranks, A1.4), 58 informative regions (130 positives, 2,518 negatives); region-clustered bootstrap (10,000, seed 20261020, percentile 95% CI) and within-region label permutation p (10,000; one-sided, "greater"; A2.1). ### 2.12 Multiple testing and unblinding protocol
P1: Holm across the four headline models (one-sided permutation p); P2: separate Holm family of four (two-sided bootstrap p) (A1.6). Everything else (sensitivities, eQTL, paired model differences, clogit) is secondary/exploratory with BH-FDR within family. Unblinding (A3): per model, only from a finalised frozen score run; AlphaGenome and Enformer were examined earlier in interim, single-model, unadjusted looks (EXP-011); Holm, paired model deltas and the framing rule were computed only in the final run with all four models complete (EXP-017); every look is logged in `research_management/EXECUTION_LOG.md`. **Framing rule (plan §7)**: B is supported only if P1 for at least one model has Holm p < 0.05, a CI excluding 0.5, and exceeds the distance baseline with the CI of the paired difference excluding 0. <!-- src: E2_ANALYSIS_PLAN.md §4, §7, A3; research_management/EXPERIMENT_REGISTRY.csv EXP-011, EXP-017 -->

### 2.13 Post hoc exploratory analyses (RV-007; EXP-018; not pre-registered)

## 4. Discussion
1. **Principal finding (variant annotation; Framing B, pre-registered).** Within Chen fine-mapping regions, all four models rank MPRA-active CCVs above MPRA-inactive CCVs better than chance (stratified AUROC 0.640-0.674, Holm p 0.0004 for each) and clearly better than distance to the nearest TSS (0.5153, at chance) <!-- src: results_phase2/e2_validation/final/tables/p1.tsv, baselines.tsv -->. This is consistent with the models capturing sequence features of regulatory activity that prioritise variants within a locus beyond proximity to a gene, in a design (within-region, region-clustered) that guards against the trivial between-locus confound. The effect is modest, the CIs are wide (lower bounds 0.554-0.607), and the check is against an episomal assay in three colon lines.
2. **What the models do not show.** (a) *No model differences:* the six headline pairwise CIs all include 0, and the study can resolve only differences of a few hundredths (minimum detectable AUROC 0.5847 against 0.5); the ordering Enformer, AlphaGenome, Borzoi-TL, pretrained Borzoi is not evidence that any model is better. (b) *Comparable to existing annotation:* the Chen annotation count alone gives 0.6182 [0.5560, 0.6845], and no model is shown to exceed it (all four paired CIs include 0), so added value over existing epigenomic annotation is not demonstrated; the models may nonetheless be recapitulating the same regulatory information. (c) *No tumour specificity:* HCEC-1CT (normal colon) labels give AUROCs similar to the tumour lines HT29 and SW403, so no tumour or "CRC-specific" signal is shown and the models are not claimed to annotate CRC specifically. (d) *Direction unresolved:* P2 concordance is below 0.5 for all four models, but with the ST4 sign convention unresolved this is uninterpretable and is not used to infer the REF/ALT convention; AlphaGenome colon RNA-seq direction against GTEx eQTL slopes is at chance overall (0.5008), without a join error, and tail-subset increases are post hoc and not a finding. (e) *Weight concentration:* 45.0% of the P1 weight sits in three regions; equal-weight and leave-region-out analyses (post hoc exploratory) give similar or higher AUROC, so the result is not driven by those regions, but about 12 effective regions support the estimate.
3. **Track benchmark as a cautionary, leakage-aware result.** For the original head-only fine-tuning recipe (Poisson-NLL MLP head on the frozen Borzoi trunk, batch size 1, lr 1e-4, 8 epochs), a ridge readout of pretrained Borzoi outputs and a linear probe on the same trunk embedding both exceed Borzoi-TL on held-out chromosomes (primary inference TF-cluster, 152 non-identical tracks; all CIs exclude 0 across tile-block, TF-cluster and cell-line-cluster units), robustly over five head seeds and folds F2-F4, within every Borzoi exposure class, and after partial exposure control of the ridge. <!-- src: results_phase2/e4_sensitivity/A_cluster_cis.csv; e5_seeds; e5_folds; SD-019 --> This was the pre-registered SD-008 outcome and withdraws any claim of Borzoi-TL superiority or CRC-specific adaptation (SD-019). TL minus pretrained Borzoi is small and not robust to cell-line clustering, and held-out-vocabulary transfer shows no advantage. The result is scoped to the single recipe tested: the probe-versus-TL gap could arise from loss, optimisation, batch size or head nonlinearity, none of which was tested, and no statement is made about transfer learning in general. Its broader lesson is methodological: at least 31 of 183 test tracks are Borzoi/Enformer training experiments and chr9/chr22 lie largely inside their training folds, so evaluations on public CRC panels must report exposure explicitly.
4. **Training exposure as a design issue.** Public CRC ChIP-seq (especially LoVo GSE51142 and ENCODE colon/HCT-116) is in the pretraining vocabularies; fold-level overlap of any "held-out" chromosome with backbone training must be reported. For the variant analysis, pretrained models saw the REF sequence of all loci and ENCODE/GTEx data; the MPRA labels are independent measurements, but the Law tiers used ATAC/ChIP annotations, GTEx RNA-seq is in model training, and AlphaGenome training exposure could not be verified. The chr1/8/9/22 subset is Borzoi-TL validation/test chromosomes only and is not a leakage test.
5. **Relation to prior work.** The closest work is li2026 (Enformer retrained on breast and prostate TF ChIP-seq, with variant and TWAS analyses); method novelty here is limited, and the added value is exposure-stratified inference, a four-model comparison under one frozen protocol, and region-clustered MPRA validation. Cross-individual variant-effect prediction is known to be limited (huang2023); the present within-locus, between-variant design is a different question.
6. **Interpretation.** Associations with MPRA activity at fine-mapped loci do not establish causal variants or mechanisms; larger predicted effects do not imply greater accuracy; Borzoi scores are on the scaled output scale, so magnitudes are not comparable across models.
7. **Proposed future work (not done; proposals only).** (i) Resolve the ST4 logFC sign convention with the MPRA authors (BL-007), after which P2 could be re-evaluated as a new, clearly labelled analysis; (ii) validate in an independent MPRA/STARR dataset (candidates are listed in `VALIDATION_AVAILABILITY.md` but were not retrieved or verified here and are not cited); (iii) obtain the AlphaGenome training manifest to settle its exposure; (iv) a model-by-annotation comparison that tests whether models add information beyond the annotation count (for example nested models in the clogit framework), and a tumour-versus-normal design with adequate power; (v) backbone fine-tuning (yuan2026-style) as a separate question from head-only fine-tuning.

## 5. Limitations (merged list; RV-007 items and earlier items deduplicated)
Test/data (track benchmark)
1. Single split for the headline analysis; the headline checkpoint is one unseeded run. Seeds 1-5 and folds F2-F4 use heads retrained on a slim cache (no RC, shift-emulated, fp16; SD-018), so they test a slightly different recipe; all nine fold x seed heads (F2/F3/F4 x seeds 1-3) are complete. No linear-probe statistics exist for F2-F4. No ablation of any recipe component and no alternative head architecture was evaluated (SD-021). The negative result is scoped to the original head-only fine-tuning recipe, and loss/optimisation as its explanation is untested. <!-- src: SD-018, SD-020, SD-021; results_phase2/e5_folds/SUMMARY.md -->
2. chr9/chr22 are development-exposed (SD-009) and about 44% (Borzoi) / 73% (Enformer) of test bp lie in pretrained training folds; chr1/chr8 validation also overlaps (66% / 90%). Chromosome-fold CV tests head robustness only, not backbone exposure. The Enformer fold file is a third-party reproduction; Borzoi HF replicate weights are assumed to share the README folds (unverified).
3. Region-level uncertainty in the track benchmark spans two test chromosomes; chromosome-level resampling is impossible.
4. The 31 identical-experiment tracks are a lower bound (GSM/ENCSR identity only; ENCODE cross-references searched for HCT116/Caco-2 only; reprocessed duplicates missed). AlphaGenome training exposure is undetermined (manifest not obtained); the AlphaGenome exclusion is a proxy.
5. Oracle mapping selects on test (upper bound); validation-selected mapping is deployable. The earlier 1,882-track "filtered Borzoi" comparison used test-selected tracks.
6. Non-independence: 183 tracks, 77 TFs, 12 cell-line/tissue labels (11 for the 152-track set); cell-line-cluster CIs rest on few clusters and are wide; identical-experiment, same-cell-line-same-target and unexposed subsets have <6 clusters and are descriptive. The sign-flip test and the CIs estimate different quantities. The 17 "unexposed" cell-line tracks test pretrained exposure, not TL generalisation. E8 rests on 4 cell lines (about 3 donor clusters if LS174T derives from LS180; unverified here [REF NEEDED]), with 94% of the gap in four LS180 tracks, a non-selection-matched comparison and the held-out tracks' validation labels used (not zero-shot).
7. Exposure control is partial: ridge features include Borzoi outputs for training tracks of at least 31 targets; the exposure-controlled ridge removes only identical-experiment and same-cell-line same-TF channels (53 tracks affected); the linear probe is the exposure-symmetric comparison; Enformer fold exposure was not assessed in the E4 sensitivity analysis. The exposure-controlled ridge and the Borzoi fold stratification were specified after the primary E4 result; fold-stratified groups are confounded with chromosome (held-out tiles 96% chr9) and signal level.
8. 51 zero-signal tiles inflate pooled Pearson r; r, r^2 and R^2 are not interchangeable; TL R^2 (uncalibrated) versus baseline validation-calibrated R^2 compare unlike objects.
9. Cell-type, resolution and context mismatch between models (524 kb/32 bp, 196 kb/128 bp, 1 Mb); the pretrained 132-track set is LoVo-heavy (118 LoVo) whereas the Borzoi-TL and AlphaGenome sets are HCT-116-heavy, and max-over-tracks favours models with more tracks (mean-|score| sensitivity pre-specified).
10. Enformer snapshot revision used in the August oracle run is unknown; the AlphaGenome hosted API varies slightly between calls (max 2.1e-4 in per-target r).
Variants and MPRA validation
11. S6 contains no alleles; alleles come from marginal trans-ancestry GWAS summary statistics (GCST90129505, harmonised with Ensembl 95/dbSNP 151), not Chen's conditional analyses. "Matched" certifies the tested allele pair only; the risk allele is by beta sign; 544 palindromic variants outside S15 rely on the harmonisation strand; 10 CCVs (23 rows) without allele data are excluded, not guessed; two rsID-drift CCVs are lost.
12. The upstream hg19-to-hg38 conversion that built the scoring input is undocumented (verified post hoc by liftover and Ensembl). The 4,396 extra ALTs at matched positions are exploratory.
13. **The MPRA is episomal** and measures activity in three colon lines (HT29, SW403, HCEC-1CT), not chromatin context. **A non-hit is not a null**: negatives are "tested, ST2 flag 0", per-variant effect sizes of non-hits are not public, and low activity or low power cannot be separated from no effect. Flag-1 variants are ambiguous and excluded from the primary.
14. **Few effective regions.** P1 uses 58 informative regions with concentrated weight (top 3 regions 45.0% of weight; effective number of regions 11.9; post hoc exploratory); LD within loci and about 143 independent regions overall; minimum detectable AUROC 0.5847. Differences between models of a few hundredths cannot be resolved ("no model differs" refers to the six headline pairs and is not equivalence).
15. **The annotation-count baseline matches the models** (0.6182 [0.5560, 0.6845]); no model is shown to exceed it, so added value over existing annotation is not shown.
16. **No tumour-versus-normal specificity**: HCEC-1CT (normal) labels give AUROCs similar to the tumour lines; no "CRC-specific" annotation claim is made.
17. **P2 is unresolved**: the Law ST4 logFC sign convention is unknown (SD-017, BL-007); the below-0.5 concordance is uninterpretable, is not used to infer the REF/ALT convention, and is excluded from the framing rule.
18. **Pretrained models saw all loci**: the REF sequence of every locus and ENCODE/GTEx data were in the training data of the pretrained models (Borzoi and Enformer; AlphaGenome unverified); the chr1/8/9/22 subset is not held out and is underpowered (19 positives, 10 regions), so it neither supports nor refutes leakage. eQTL checks are only partly independent (GTEx RNA-seq is in training; GTEx v10 sign concordance with ALT/REF 0.57, p 0.17, gives no sign information).
19. **Score conventions**: AlphaGenome quantile scores are provider-defined and tie-heavy (97.5% ties; 254 distinct values over all rows; midranks; raw log2 is the tie-free sensitivity); AlphaGenome RC averaging is server-side. Borzoi scores are summed in the scaled/soft-clipped output scale, and pseudocount conventions (pc = 1 on aggregated sums; server-side epsilon for AlphaGenome) differ between models. 512 bp versus 501 bp windows and bin granularity (Enformer 128 bp bins blur the variant position) differ across models. Chromosome-end windows: 149 (Borzoi) / 30 (Enformer) rows N-padded, 242 AlphaGenome rows shifted (S4 excludes them).
20. **Post hoc analyses** (EXP-018) were defined after the final unblinding, use the same data, and have no multiplicity control; the clogit likelihood-ratio p values assume independent variants and are not quoted as evidence strength; eQTL tail-subset concordances are not findings.
21. No causal, clinical or mechanistic claims.
Process
22. Interim looks at AlphaGenome and Enformer preceded the final run (EXP-011; Holm and the framing rule were computed only in the final run, A3). The final E2 run (EXP-017) was independently reviewed (RV-007 PASS). Some track-benchmark state-file results (EXP-005/006/007/010) are listed in the registry as "reviewer pending".
